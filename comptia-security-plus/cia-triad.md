# 📘 Lesson 2: The CIA Triad — CompTIA Security+ (Professor Messer)

This lesson covers the **CIA Triad**, a foundational model in cybersecurity representing the three core principles of protecting information: **Confidentiality**, **Integrity**, and **Availability**. Mastering this triad is essential for understanding how security policies and mechanisms are structured, especially for the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) exam.

---

## 🔐 What Is the CIA Triad?

The CIA Triad is a model used to guide policies and practices for securing data and systems. It ensures information is:

- **Confidential**: Only accessible to those with proper authorization.
- **Accurate and Intact** (*Integrity*): Not altered maliciously or accidentally.
- **Available**: Accessible to users when needed.

---

## 🛡️ Core Components of the CIA Triad

| Principle       | Description                                                                 | Examples                                      |
|----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| **Confidentiality** | Prevents unauthorized access to sensitive data.                          | Encryption, access control, MFA               |
| **Integrity**       | Ensures data is not tampered with or altered improperly.                 | Hashing, digital signatures, checksums        |
| **Availability**    | Ensures systems and data are accessible when needed.                     | Backups, redundancy, system patching          |

---

## 🧭 Common Threats to Each Principle

| CIA Element     | Common Threats                             |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Confidentiality** | Eavesdropping, data breaches, insider threats |
| **Integrity**       | Data tampering, man-in-the-middle attacks     |
| **Availability**    | DDoS attacks, hardware failures, ransomware    |

---

## 📌 Key Takeaways

- The CIA Triad is the **baseline** for security policy design and risk management.
- A **balanced approach** is critical — over-focusing on one area (e.g. availability) can compromise the others.
- Most security tools and practices support at least one element of the triad.
- Understanding how to **apply and evaluate** the CIA principles is crucial for the Security+ exam and real-world scenarios.
